Pexels licence notes
Source: https://www.pexels.com/license/ (page fetched once, 2026-11-07, titled "Free Stock Photo & Video License - Pexels")

ALLOWED (as stated on the page)
- All photos and videos on Pexels can be downloaded and used for free.
- Attribution is not required (crediting the photographer or Pexels is "always appreciated").
- Photos and videos may be modified / edited (cropping, resizing and re-encoding to WebP counts as modification).
- Listed use cases: websites, blogs, apps, e-commerce shops, newsletters, e-books, presentations, templates you sell;
  ads, banners and marketing campaigns; print marketing material (flyers, postcards, invitations, magazines, books);
  sharing on social media.

NOT ALLOWED (as stated on the page)
- Identifiable people may not appear in a bad light or in a way that is offensive.
- Do not sell unaltered copies of a photo or video (e.g. as a poster, print or on a physical product) without modifying it first.
- Do not imply endorsement of your product by people or brands shown in the imagery.
- Do not redistribute or sell the photos/videos on other stock photo or wallpaper platforms.
- Do not use the photos/videos as part of a trademark, design-mark, trade name, business name or service mark.

IMPLICATIONS FOR THE KORA CATALOGUE
- Using the photos as website imagery for client sites is within the stated "website, blog or app" use.
- Photos with identifiable people were avoided or are only accepted where the person is neutral/unidentifiable; the catalogue
  must not present any pictured person as a customer, owner or endorser of a Kora client.
- The catalogue should not be exposed as a standalone downloadable photo library (that would approach redistribution).
- Do not use any photo inside a client's logo or business mark.
- Attribution is optional; creator names and Pexels source URLs are still recorded per photo in catalog-additions-A.json.

CAVEAT
- The licence text above is the general Pexels licence page. Per-photo "Free to use" status was inferred from the photo being served
  on Pexels search pages (which state "Completely Free to Use"); individual photo pages were not fetched (www.pexels.com blocks curl and
  the scrape budget was limited). Sponsored (Getty/iStock) results on the search pages were ignored.
