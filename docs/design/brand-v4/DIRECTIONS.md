# Kora Brand V4: M2 exploration (prototype, not shipped)

Sheet: `sheet.svg` / `sheet.png` (rendered with resvg; three directions drawn quickly to test the concept → object → motion → vocabulary chain borrowed from willie).

|                 | Concept                                 | Object                    | Motion                    | Interface verb     |
| --------------- | --------------------------------------- | ------------------------- | ------------------------- | ------------------ |
| A Resonance     | A business is a signal others recognise | offset arcs + core        | arcs settle outward       | "Send your signal" |
| B Level line    | Calm control extended to growth         | line with one marker      | dip and return            | "Keep it level"    |
| C Mise en place | Everything laid out, ready to serve     | four tiles, one displaced | tiles reflow into layouts | "Lay it out"       |

## Review (looking at the rendered sheet)

- **A fails the mandate's own rule.** As drawn it reads as a Wi-Fi icon, which is the "stock soundwave" cliché the brief bans. The Resonance idea survives as a concept, not as this mark. Rejected as drawn.
- **B reads as a heartbeat/ECG glyph** and belongs to the reliability story only. Kept as the Proof-mode motif, not the company mark.
- **C is the only one that is not already a generic icon**, and it matches what Kora now does: it composes layouts, campaigns and assets. Its weakness: four rounded squares are also generic until the displaced tile and motion carry it.

## Decision (provisional, revisit at M4 with real page context)

Carry **C forward**, with these ownable rules to be tested next:

1. The displaced tile is the brand code; it appears in every output as a deliberate offset of one element.
2. Motion: tiles reflow into the page's actual layout (the product demo is the logo animation).
3. Palette candidate: ink `#14121f`, warm paper `#f4f1ea`, ultramarine `#2b3cff`, one hot accent `#ff5a3c`. Green and gold are dropped. This accent is near willie's orange-red; M4 must pick a distinct hue before shipping.
4. Reliability keeps B (level line) as its Proof-mode motif.

## Not done / limits

- No production UI uses this yet; `docs/design/brand.md` ("Calm control") remains the live brand until M3/M4 ship the shell and public page.
- No audience testing; the choice is a design judgment, not evidence.
