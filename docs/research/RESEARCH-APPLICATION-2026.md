# How the 2026 research was applied

Sources: `COMPETITOR-ANALYSIS-2026.md`, `CATEGORY-DESIGN-STUDY-A-2026.md`, `CATEGORY-DESIGN-STUDY-B-2026.md` and their JSON twins in
`data/`. All read 2026-11-07. **Limits that apply to every row:** the studies used page-text fetches, so typography, colour,
sticky behaviour and true phone layout were not observed; the photographer and medical categories and the small-chops
category rest on very few readable sites; Webflow facts are from search summaries. The findings are structural principles, not
designs to copy, and none of them overrides the rule that Kora never invents business facts.

Status key: **Changed** = code changed in this work because of the finding (with the test or check that proves it);
**Already so** = the engine already behaved this way, checked against generated output; **Not adopted** = deliberately left out.

## From the category studies

| Finding (category, support)                                                                                            | Status            | What exists / why not                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| One contact action stays reachable at any scroll depth on a phone (small chops 2/3, barber 4/4, home repair 3/3)       | **Changed**       | `dock-policy.ts` (`wantsDock`): every contact-led trade now gets the quick-contact dock on all three directions, previously only designs with the `action-dock` genome. Photographers and studios (view-work intent) keep the quiet layout. Test: `vnext.test.ts` "category-study rule". The critic's two mobile warnings use the same predicate.                                        |
| Without reviews, trust comes from process, provenance and enquiry guidance, never invented numbers (all 14 categories) | **Already so**    | Provenance tags, claim validator and whole-site gate; no stat bands, ratings or logos are ever generated. Matrix: 0 blocking issues across 23 businesses × 3 directions.                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Tell the customer what to send: date, guests, occasion, allergies, photo idea (bakery 2/3, small chops 3/3, cakes)     | **Already so**    | Bakery/caterer pages generate "What to include when you get in touch" and a FAQ; lead times are asked about, never claimed (checked on the Sweet Crumbs output).                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Medical: emergency notice; no outcome or credential claims; no testimonials as efficacy proof                          | **Already so**    | Medical profile copy sends urgent problems to emergency services and forbids credential/outcome claims (`knowledge.ts`); the matrix medical site contains the notice. Registration numbers are not generated.                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Never render an empty section (photographer, 1/3 negative example)                                                     | **Already so**    | `completenessV3` blocks empty sections and the repair loop removes them.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Short navigation (4-7 items), plain task-named labels                                                                  | **Already so**    | 4-7 pages, `navLabel`; the editor enforces the seven-page ceiling.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| With no clients or awards, show process and clearly labelled own work; never invent logos (agency, consultancy)        | **Already so**    | Kochab renders an "approach" page and no client logos, awards or testimonials; the claim validator rejects them.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Photograph-led, text-light layout for photographers (2/3)                                                              | **Partly**        | A dedicated gallery page and a weddings/portfolio page are generated, and the page copy is short. Only one of the three directions opens with a full-bleed photograph (`cinematic`); the other two open with a statement or type-led hero. Not changed in this work; the three directions are deliberately different and the study has weak support (3 sites, no enquiry flow observed). |
| Walk-ins welcome, 24/7 cover, guarantees, years, awards, "from" prices, delivery areas                                 | **Not adopted**   | These are facts only an owner can state. They appear only if the owner supplies them (price edits require owner confirmation).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Mega-menus, product grids, search, quick-buy, newsletter incentives (fashion)                                          | **Not adopted**   | Kora generates 4-7 page brochure sites; a shop front is out of scope. Fashion sites get an offer page, a gallery and an enquiry path.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Online booking widgets / reservation forms                                                                             | **Not adopted**   | No booking integration exists; the contact path is phone, WhatsApp, email and the enquiry form. Claiming "Book now" without a booking system would be false.                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Review walls, star ratings, stat bands (education, real estate, salons)                                                | **Not adopted**   | Would require owner-supplied, dated, verifiable data; Kora has no such feed.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Typography, colour and phone-layout rules                                                                              | **Not derivable** | Not observable by text fetch (stated in both studies). The existing design genomes and the rendered-screenshot reviews are the only evidence for these.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |

## From the competitor study

| Principle (id in the study)                                | Status                | What exists / why not                                                                                                                                       |
| ---------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| P3 structure before pixels, P4 bounded first set           | **Already so**        | The Business Blueprint proposes 4-7 pages with reasons before composition; the editor can add/remove up to seven.                                           |
| P6 edit locally, not by regenerating; undo                 | **Already so**        | Per-section ops, per-section/page regeneration, immutable versions and restore. Whole-site regeneration is never needed to change a headline.               |
| P8 AI/placeholder imagery is a labelled, replaceable layer | **Already so**        | "Photography on this site is illustrative." line, photographer credits, one-step picture replacement from Kora photos, owner photos or user-selected stock. |
| P9 say generated text must be reviewed before publishing   | **Changed**           | The editor now shows a "read every page before you publish" note beside Publish (`review-note`).                                                            |
| P10 limits are legible and degrade safely                  | **Already so**        | Media quota exhaustion falls back to the catalogue; model failure falls back to deterministic copy; a published site is never taken down by either.         |
| P13 SEO basics per page, reviewable                        | **Already so**        | Per-page title/description, editable through the `seo` and `page-meta` ops.                                                                                 |
| P7 free manual lane vs metered AI lane                     | **Already so**        | All editing and regeneration here is deterministic and unmetered; the only metered call is the optional single model composition.                           |
| P2 accept URL/file evidence instead of more typing         | **Not adopted**       | Would need fetching third-party pages (a new attack surface) and a provenance model for scraped facts. Left as follow-up.                                   |
| P17 export/handoff                                         | **Not adopted**       | Artifacts are static files in `site_versions`; no export route was built in this work.                                                                      |
| Cost: competitors bundle hosting/CRM cheaply (risk list)   | **Not a code matter** | Commercial positioning; recorded in the study's risk section for the product owner.                                                                         |
