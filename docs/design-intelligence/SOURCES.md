# Kora runtime design intelligence sources

Kora selects validated local guidance using `selectDesignRules` from `@kora/creative`. Approved sources and pinned commits are enumerated in `third_party/manifest.json`. The source files are not fetched or executed in production. Security, accessibility and customer-approved brand constraints take precedence over optional aesthetics. Selected design effects must be reflected in `planSiteV3` and its validated output; unused rules alone do not count as integration. Underlying business truth comes from the customer's brief and Brand OS.

These integrations are original Kora rules inspired by permissively licensed published guidance, not verbatim redistribution or comprehensive imports of the full upstream skills. More complete skill parity, regression screenshots and licensed redistribution audit require separate validation.
